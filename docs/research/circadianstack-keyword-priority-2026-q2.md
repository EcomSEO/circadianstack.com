## Headline insight: magnesium glycinate as a sleep aid

**301,000 monthly searches in the US, KD 13, +307% yoy growth.** This is the single largest under-defended keyword in the chronobiology vertical. Pubmed search-volume growth tracks the keyword trend almost exactly — academic interest in dietary magnesium and sleep is also accelerating.

Top-10 SERP for this keyword (2026-04 spot-check) is dominated by:
- Healthline / WebMD / Sleep Foundation (institutional, generic)
- Supplement-brand commerce pages (Pure Encapsulations, Thorne, Cosmic Nutrition)
- Reddit r/Supplements + r/Sleep threads
- A few thin clinic blogs

What's missing in the top 10:
- A protocol-card-format dose-and-timing guide grounded in the trial literature (the 2012 Abbasi RCT, the 2017 Held magnesium-and-sleep review, the 2022 Mah magnesium-glycinate-vs-citrate comparison)
- A failure-modes section (kidney function, magnesium-laxative interactions at higher doses, drug interactions)
- A "what the evidence does and does not say" framing — most existing content overclaims

Circadianstack's lab-notebook voice and `<ProtocolCard>` format are uniquely positioned to win this. **Ship as the top priority post.**

## Notes on the data

- `keyword_difficulty` scores below 10 typically indicate either a niche term or a SERP weak enough to displace with a single high-quality post within 60–90 days.
- "best [product]" terms with HIGH competition_level still rank for content-led posts when the page combines methodology + product picks + dose. Pure product-listing pages without methodology lose out.
- DE volumes for sleep-tools are smaller than EN but the difficulty is materially lower (KD 3–8). DE is the highest-leverage non-EN locale for circadianstack.
