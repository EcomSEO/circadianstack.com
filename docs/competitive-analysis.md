# CircadianStack — Competitive Landscape Analysis

**Prepared:** April 2026
**Scope:** SERP analysis + competitor landscape + 10 positioning moves for circadianstack.com.
**Target audience:** "Jamie, 30, high-agency knowledge worker" (primary) + "Dana, 46, peri-menopausal poor sleeper" (secondary) + "Vik, 34, shift worker" (tertiary).
**Source data:** DataForSEO April 2026 via `docs/circadianstack-research.md` + live SERP observations.

**Headline finding:** The circadian/sleep SERP is bifurcated. Head terms ("circadian rhythm" 110k/KD 78, "red light therapy" 60k/KD 58) are dominated by SleepFoundation, Healthline, Cleveland Clinic, Mayo — high-authority but clinical-detached, generic, and structurally weak on actionable protocols. Long-tail terms (KD ≤25) are undefended. CircadianStack's strategy is to build from the long-tail up with Protocol cards + dose-accurate product testing + the Chronotype Quiz as the audience-acquisition flywheel.

## Part 3 — 10 positioning moves

### 1. Protocol card on every how-to post
The site's defining artifact. 5 sections (Input / Dose / Expected output / Evidence / Failure modes). Screenshot-shareable. No competitor ships this format.

### 2. Dose-accurate product testing
For light therapy lamps: measured lux at 12"/18"/24" — NOT manufacturer-claimed lux. This is Wirecutter-grade methodology applied to circadian products, which Wirecutter itself hasn't done.

### 3. The Chronotype Quiz as lead magnet flywheel
MCTQ-short (peer-reviewed) + client-side scoring + personalized 7-day Protocol card PDF. Email capture on the PDF delivery step. This is the primary audience-acquisition mechanism.

### 4. Primary-source citations, not podcast citations
Khalsa 2003, Duffy 2015, Czeisler, Panda, Walker. When Huberman covers something, the citation goes to the underlying study, not to the podcast. This is the E-E-A-T signal that distinguishes us from the Huberman-adjacent content ecosystem.

### 5. Dark-mode-first design
Readable at 6am and 11pm. A design signal that the product is built for circadian use, not for 2pm-browsing lifestyle content. Also: reduced eyestrain = trust signal = loyalty.

### 6. Time-stamped dose + timing (not vague guidance)
"1,000-10,000 lux for 10-30 min within 60 min of wake" — not "get some morning light." Every recommendation has a measurable dose and a specific time window.

### 7. Edge Cases hub for the underserved audiences
Shift workers, perimenopausal women, new parents, ADHD-with-DSPD readers. The head-term SERPs don't serve them; generic sleep content assumes a 7am wake. Our edge-case protocols capture long-tail volume that competitors can't (easily) serve.

### 8. "Dose accurate" as a tagline-level promise
Everything on the site — protocols, product reviews, quiz output — carries a dose. The opposite of vibes-based wellness content. This becomes a recognizable brand signal.

### 9. No display ads
Affiliate-only monetization preserves clean reading experience + Core Web Vitals + trust signal. Every circadian-adjacent site runs display ads; not running them becomes a differentiator.

### 10. Protocol Pro subscription (post-month-8)
$8/mo for weekly personalized Protocol card. Validates paid product-market fit. Recurring revenue layered on top of affiliate. Compounds as the library grows.

## Part 4 — Risks

### Competitive
- **Huberman launches a product / premium content.** Low risk but possible. Mitigation: compete on editorial rigor + Protocol card format, not on audience size.
- **Oura / Whoop launch deeper editorial content.** Possible but owned-media bias is hard for them to overcome.
- **Wirecutter adds dose-accuracy to light therapy testing.** Possible. Our moat then becomes Protocol cards + chronotype quiz + edge cases.

### Platform
- **Core update weighting YMYL on sleep content.** Mitigation: medical disclaimers on all clinical-claims posts; primary sources on every claim.
- **Pinterest / social algorithm changes.** CircadianStack is mostly Google + Twitter + Reddit — Pinterest is tertiary. Low exposure.
- **Quiz-related schema changes.** Mitigation: use standard SoftwareApplication schema; don't over-depend on rich results for quiz traffic.

### Editorial
- **Published sleep research updates.** Frequent. Mitigation: quarterly pillar reviews, dated revision notes.
- **Melatonin dose controversy deepens.** We've staked a position at 0.3-0.5mg based on current evidence. If new trial data reverses this, we update with dated revision.
- **Mouth tape sleep — research evolving.** Covered cautiously with evidence-review framing. Easy to update.

## Bottom line

CircadianStack wins the long-tail first, then compounds authority from there. The head terms ("circadian rhythm," "jet lag," "red light therapy") are aspirational — we target them via cluster posts and Protocol cards that rank for sub-terms. The Protocol card format + dose-accurate testing + MCTQ-based Chronotype Quiz are three stackable differentiators that no competitor in the space currently ships.

The slowest-ramping site in the network (circadian is a long-compound vertical), but also the highest long-term retention — once Jamie finds us, he comes back monthly. Protocol Pro validates the recurring-revenue thesis.
