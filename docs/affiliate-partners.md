## 8. What we won't affiliate

- Any supplement sold as a "sleep stack" without evidence (i.e., no random nootropic brand stacks)
- Any "biohacker" product with weak third-party testing
- Melatonin brands at supraphysiological doses (>1mg) marketed for daily use — we can mention but don't recommend
- Red light panels sold with "heals-everything" marketing (covered in Light hub but only within evidence-backed use cases)
- Grounding / earthing mats (evidence too thin)
- CBD / cannabis for sleep (legal patchwork + evidence varies)
