## Strategic notes

**The Protocol card is the signature artifact.** Every how-to post has one. Screenshot-shareable on Twitter. That's how we compete with Huberman-lane content — the card is cleaner than their podcast snippets.

**The Chronotype Quiz is the lead magnet.** Not a PDF, not an ebook — a quiz with client-side scoring and a personalized output PDF. Email capture happens at the "email me my personalized protocol" step, not at the quiz entry.

**Seasonal balance:** Hub 1 (light therapy) peaks in Q4/Q1 (winter). Hub 5 (jet lag, shift work) is year-round. Hub 3 (chronotype) grows steadily (search volume rises year-over-year). Hub 2 (sleep architecture) is evergreen. Hub 4 (tools) peaks in Q4 (gift guide season).

**Cross-site angle:** CircadianStack is independent from the peptide + nutrition + non-toxic clusters. It shares infrastructure but not content. The only meaningful cross-link is occasional — when plasticfreelab discusses microplastics in bedding, we might link to circadianstack's mattress-related content, but this is rare and deliberate.
